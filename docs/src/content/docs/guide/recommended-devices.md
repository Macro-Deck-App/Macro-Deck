title: Recommended devices

---

Looking for a device to use as your deck? These are real devices and how well they work. For the
general rules behind them, which systems the web client and the companion app run on, see
[Compatibility](/guide/compatibility/).

🟢 recommended · 🟡 works, with caveats · 🔴 not recommended

| Device | OS | Recommended | Notes |
| --- | --- | :---: | --- |
| iPad Air 2 | iOS 15 | 🟡 | Works great with both the companion app and the web client, but long-term compatibility cannot be guaranteed. |
| Echo Show (1st gen) | Android | 🟡 | Works great with the companion app and the web client, but requires a jailbreak. |
| iPad 2 | iOS 9 | 🔴 | No companion app, and the web client is really slow. |

## Add your device

Using Macro Deck on a device that is not listed? Click **Edit page** at the bottom of this page,
add a row for your device to the table and open a pull request with your change.
